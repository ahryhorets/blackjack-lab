5. Strategy lab: how the simulator tests work

The simulator (python3 sim.py) plays bots through exactly the same table.py round code you play through. Only the decision-maker changes. For each strategy it plays N rounds from a shuffled 6-deck shoe and measures:

  • Edge = total won ÷ total wagered. -0.5% means you lose 50 cents per 100 bet, on average.
  • 95% range = edge ± 1.96 × SD ÷ √N. One round has SD ≈ 1.15 bets, so after 200,000 rounds the range is still about ±0.45%. That's why the basic strategy result (-0.17%) is "consistent with -0.4%".
  • Max drawdown: deepest fall from a peak. Ruined: balance fell below the minimum bet.

The players

counter + count betsCounter's play, and bets 1 unit at TC < 2, then 2 units per true count, max 12 units. Bets big only when the shoe favours the player.+0.93%
mimicPlays like the dealer: hit below 17, never double or split.-5.74%
never_bustStands on any hard 12+, so it never busts.-6.28%
always_hitHits until bust or 21.-64.6%
martingaleBasic play, but doubles the bet after every loss and resets after a win. Bankroll 2,000.ruined in 425 rounds

Why each result comes out the way it does

  • The house edge comes from one rule: if you bust, you lose, even if the dealer busts later. Everything else is symmetric or favours you (3:2 blackjack, doubling, splitting, surrender).
  • Mimic the dealer keeps that disadvantage and gives up all your extras: -5.7%.
  • Never bust avoids the double-bust trap, but standing on 12-16 against a 7-A loses ~50-55% of the time, and the dealer only busts ~23% vs a ten: -6.3%.
  • Basic strategy uses every option at the right moment and cuts the edge to about -0.4%. That's the most you can get without tracking cards.
  • Counting with a flat bet barely helps: the deviations add about 0.1-0.2%. The money in counting comes from the bet spread: small bets in bad shoes, big bets in good ones. Average edge per hand is still slightly negative, but weighted by bet size it turns positive (+0.93%).
  • Martingale doesn't change the edge at all (every bet is still -0.4%). It just stacks big bets onto losing streaks. Seven losses in a row (about 1 in 100 sequences) needs 1,280 for the next bet, and the table max of 1,000 makes recovery impossible. Many small wins, one huge loss.

Rerun any of it: python3 sim.py --compare 50000, python3 sim.py --play counter --bet count --bankroll 2000, python3 sim.py --cutcard 100000 (deeper cut card = more high-count hands = bigger counter edge).
